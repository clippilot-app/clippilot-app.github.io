Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information ClipPilot (the Windows app and its account server) collects, why, and the choices you have. ClipPilot is made by voxella voice ("we", "us"). If you have a question, email ClipPilot.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your projects, scripts, voice recordings, thumbnails and media files stay on your computer or phone. We never store them on our servers. The only exception is the speech-to-text step of auto captions and auto-cut, which sends short pieces of sound to our server to be turned into words and keeps nothing.
  • Editing AI tools like background removal, auto-reframe, studio sound, auto-cut’s silence detection and LUTs run entirely on your device.
  • If you sign in, we keep your name, email address and Google account ID so you can have an account and a paid plan.
  • The app sends anonymous usage reports (which you can turn off) so we can count active users.
  • YouTube and Google Drive features talk directly between your PC and Google. The only YouTube information that reaches our server is your channel's public profile if you use Creator Chat, and the channel totals BEAN may see.
  • If you use Creator Chat, the messages you send are stored on our server so the other person can read them.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Information we collect

Your ClipPilot account (optional)

When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, email address and Google account ID (we ask only for the basic "openid email profile" permissions). We store these with the date you signed up and when you last used ClipPilot. Your session is stored on your PC; our server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it, and it expires after 30 days.

Payments

Paid plans are processed by Paystack. Your card details go to Paystack, never to us. We keep the payment reference, plan, amount, currency, status and dates, plus the Paystack customer and subscription codes, so we can give you the plan you paid for and keep accounting records.

Anonymous usage reports

Unless you turn them off (Settings → Account), the app sends a report about every 10 minutes while it is open. It contains a random ID for that copy of ClipPilot, the app version and edition, the Windows version, and how many times a few features were used (for example the video editor, export, Voice Studio, BEAN, cloud backup and analytics). It never contains your projects, file names, scripts, YouTube data or anything you type. If you are signed in, the report is linked to your account so we can see active accounts. To stop fake reports, our server keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your network address for up to 2 days; it never stores the address itself.

BEAN, the in-app assistant

When you chat with BEAN, the app sends your message and the information BEAN needs to answer to our server: for example project and video names, plans, scripts, timeline details, thumbnail layers, and, if YouTube is connected, your channel's subscriber, view and video totals. Our server passes this to Cloudflare Workers AI to produce the answer and returns it to your PC. We do not store your conversations; we keep only how many BEAN messages each account sent each day (and the size of those requests) to apply daily limits. Your conversation history stays on your PC.

Your BEAN chat history (so you can reopen old chats) is saved on your own device, not on our server. You can delete any saved chat from BEAN's History. When BEAN uses an editing tool for you, it asks your permission first; it always asks before anything that changes your YouTube channel.

Auto captions, auto-cut and the Shorts maker (optional)

When you use auto captions, or auto-cut's filler-word and retake detection, the app takes only the sound of the parts of your video you use, as small pieces of low-quality mono audio, and sends them to our server, which passes them to Cloudflare Workers AI for speech-to-text and returns the words with their timings. The audio is not stored, by us or by the AI service, and is never used to train AI models. The Shorts maker and channel coach send the transcript text (not the video) the same way. We keep only how many caption minutes each account used each day. Local copies the app makes for this are deleted from your device after a day.

On-device AI tools

Background removal, auto-reframe, studio sound, loudness mastering, silence detection, LUT colour looks, text to speech and the thumbnail cutout run on your own device using bundled AI models and FFmpeg. Nothing from these tools is sent to us.

Creator Chat (optional)

Creator Chat lets signed-in ClipPilot users message each other. Your chat profile is your YouTube channel. When you set it up (or click Refresh from YouTube), the app sends your YouTube access key to our server once, and our server asks YouTube which channel it belongs to (the YouTube "read only" permission). We keep only your channel's public details: the channel ID, name, @handle, profile picture link and public subscriber count (if the channel shows it), plus whether you want to appear in search. The access key is used for that one request and is never stored on our server. During our November 2026 launch offer, the ClipPilot Support account may send you one welcome message in Creator Chat.

If you message the ClipPilot Support account, the latest messages in that chat (up to 12) may be sent to Cloudflare Workers AI so it can answer simple questions straight away. These answers are marked “automatic reply”. They aren’t used to train AI models, and a person from the team handles anything about payments, refunds or your account.

We store the chat messages and photos you send (the app shrinks photos before sending them), who they were sent to and when, which messages each person has read, chat requests (accepted or declined), people you block, and reports you send, so the chat can work and we can deal with abuse. Your email address is never shown to other users. The owner of ClipPilot may read a reported conversation's messages to handle the report.

Voice messages, stickers and calls. Voice messages you record are stored on our server like photos, so the other person can play them. Stickers are ClipPilot's own pictures; only the sticker's name is stored. Voice and video calls, including screen sharing, go directly between the two apps (WebRTC, encrypted). Their sound, video and screen never pass through or get stored on our server. To connect a call, our server briefly passes the technical connection details between the two apps and keeps a record of the call: who called whom, when it started, answered and ended, and how it ended (for example declined or missed). A short line such as “Voice call · 2:31” appears in the chat. If your networks can't connect directly, the call may be relayed through Cloudflare's call relay, which still can't see inside it. Your microphone, camera and screen are only used while you record or are in a call, and you choose which screen or window to share. Soundboard sounds you import stay on your PC.

Your own stickers. Stickers you make from your pictures are kept on your PC. When you send one, it is stored on our server like a photo (and deleted after 60 days).

Screenshot notices. If someone takes a screenshot while your chat is open on their screen, the chat shows a short notice (for example “📸 Alex took a screenshot of the chat”), stored like a message. On Windows, ClipPilot notices new files appearing in the Screenshots folders while a chat is open; it never opens, reads or uploads the screenshot itself. On Android 14 and newer, Android tells the app a screenshot was taken. You can hide these notices in Chat settings.

Group chats and replies. You can make group chats of up to 25 people and add anyone you already have an accepted chat with. Everyone in a group sees its name, its members (their channel name and picture) and the messages sent there, including messages sent before they joined. Online status and activity in a group are shown only to people who already have an accepted 1:1 chat with you. When you reply to a message, your message stores which message it answers, and a short preview of that message is shown with it. Leaving a group stops new messages reaching you; the messages you sent stay in the group. When the last person leaves, the group and its messages are deleted.

Reactions, edits, pins and forwarding. Reactions you add to messages (an emoji or a ClipPilot sticker) are stored on our server and shown to everyone in that chat, with your channel name. When you edit a message, the new text replaces the old one and the message shows "(edited)". Deleting a message removes it for everyone. Pinned messages are visible to everyone in the chat. Forwarding sends a copy of the message into the chat you choose, marked "Forwarded". Bookmarks and reminders you set are kept only on your device.

Chats saved on your device. So your earlier messages still open when you're offline, ClipPilot keeps a copy of your recent chats (up to the last 150 messages of each) in the app's storage on your PC or phone. This copy never leaves your device, and signing in with a different account doesn't show it.

Calls through a relay. When two devices can't connect to each other directly (common on mobile data and work networks), call audio and video pass through a relay server (TURN) run by a relay provider. It is encrypted end to end between the devices, so the relay can't hear or see the call, and nothing is recorded.

Group calls. Anyone in a group chat can start a voice call there, and up to 8 members can be in it at once; each person can turn on their camera or share their screen. Call audio and video go directly between the devices in the call (or through the relay above), encrypted end to end, and are never recorded. Our server only passes along the connection details and keeps a record of who was in the call, when, and for how long, which members of the group can see in the chat.

Shared project folders. You can share a ClipPilot project with up to 9 other people: people you already chat with, or anyone signed in to ClipPilot with Creator Chat who opens your invite link or scans its QR code. The invite code is part of the link after the "#", so our website never receives it; our server stores the code, which project it belongs to and when it was made or turned off, and the list of people the owner removed so they can't rejoin through the link. To keep everyone on the same version, the project file (its timeline, text and settings, not your video, audio or image files) is stored on our server, together with who saved each version and when; we keep the last 10 versions. Your media files are sent directly from one member's PC to another's, encrypted, only while ClipPilot is open on both, and are not stored on our server. The owner can remove people or stop sharing at any time, which deletes the shared copy from our server; each person's copy on their own PC stays.

Online status and activity. While ClipPilot is open on your desktop, it keeps a live connection to our server so messages and calls arrive instantly. People you have an accepted chat with can see whether you are online, idle, on Do Not Disturb or offline (choose Invisible to appear offline). If “Show the app or game I’m using” is on (you can turn it off in Creator Chat → your profile), the app checks on your PC which well-known app or game is open, such as Roblox or Glasswave, and shares only its name (for example “Playing Roblox”) with those same people. It never shares window titles, files or what you do inside the app. Your chosen status is stored on our server; your activity is kept only while you are online.

Visiting this website

When you open a page on this website, it stores a random ID in your browser's local storage and sends us that ID, the page you opened and the website that linked you here (its address only). Our server adds the country your connection comes from, as reported by Cloudflare, and the kind of device and browser you use. We use this only to count how many people visit and where they come from. We don't use cookies and don't share this with anyone. Your IP address isn't stored; to stop abuse we keep only a scrambled code made from it, which changes every day and is deleted after 2 days. It is turned off if your browser sends "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control, and you can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Information we don't collect

We don't collect your media files, voice recordings, exported videos, thumbnails, project files, YouTube passwords, card numbers or contacts.

Who we share information with

We share information only with services that run ClipPilot for us, and only what they need:

  • Cloudflare hosts our account server and database (including Creator Chat messages), and runs the AI models for BEAN and speech-to-text.
  • Other ClipPilot users you chat with see your chat profile (your YouTube channel's public details) and the messages you send them.
  • Paystack processes payments.
  • Google provides sign-in, and the YouTube and Drive features you choose to use.
  • GitHub hosts the ClipPilot installer and app updates.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect ClipPilot and its users from fraud or abuse.

How long we keep information

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Usage reports and daily BEAN counts: about 13 months.
  • Website visit counts: about 13 months after your last visit.
  • Creator Chat profile and messages: until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your chat profile, the messages and photos you sent, your read status and your blocks.
  • Chat photos and voice messages: 60 days, then they are deleted (the message shows "Photo expired" or "Voice message expired").
  • Call records (who, when, how long): up to 13 months. Calls themselves are never recorded.
  • Shared projects: the latest 10 versions, until the owner stops sharing or deletes the project, or deletes their account.
  • Free trials: which trials an account has used and when, for as long as the account exists (so each trial can be used once).
  • Chat reports: kept until they are dealt with, then for up to 13 months.
  • Sessions: until you sign out, or 30 days.
  • Payment records: as long as accounting and tax rules require. If you delete your account, they are no longer linked to you.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn usage reports off in Settings → Account.
  • Turn website visit counting off with "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control in your browser.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account (cancel any paid plan first). This removes your account, sessions, history, BEAN counts, chat profile and the chat messages you sent from our server.
  • In Creator Chat you can hide yourself from search, decline chat requests, and block or report anyone.
  • Disconnect YouTube or Google Drive in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing ClipPilot.support@gmail.com. If you are in Nigeria, you also have rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, including the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS. Sign-in sessions are stored as scrambled (hashed) values, secret keys stay in the server's protected settings, and access keys on your PC are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your Google account protected.

Children

ClipPilot is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. YouTube has its own age rules.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will also tell you in the app.
