The tour

From idea
to uploaded.

Every step of making a video lives in the same place, and each one hands off to the next. Your thumbnail and your title are already waiting when you hit Publish.

Everything in one place

Five tools.
One app.

Thumbnail editor

Photoshop-style layers, text with thick outlines, drop shadows and glow, shapes, brush and eraser, blend modes, picture adjustments and full undo history. Drop a photo in, add the big words, done.

Publish to YouTube

Upload the video and set its thumbnail straight from ClipPilot, with a progress bar and schedule support.

Project planner

Pick a format like commentary, short or tutorial and get a checklist with honest times, an outline and a script space.

Video editor

Three layouts (Simple, Motion and Studio), 81 transitions with live previews, text effects, keyframes on anything, colour grading, green screen and auto-duck.

Voice studio

Record and clean up voiceovers, then drop them on the timeline.

Channel analytics

Connect your channel to see subscribers, views, watch time, traffic sources and your top videos, right next to what you're making now.
